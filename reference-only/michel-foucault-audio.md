# Michel Foucault audio

Foucault recordings are useful human-voice references, but they are not treated
as redistributable public-domain fixtures here.

Useful archives include:

- Berkeley audio material catalogued by michel-foucault.com:
  https://michel-foucault.com/2019/12/27/archive-michel-foucault-audio-archive-2009/
- Collège de France lecture recordings:
  https://www.college-de-france.fr/en/news/michel-foucault-lectures-online

The Collège de France publication notes authorization by Foucault's heirs and
Éditions du Seuil. Keep source links and possible timestamps here; do not copy
the recordings into `audio/original/` without a recording-specific rights
check.
