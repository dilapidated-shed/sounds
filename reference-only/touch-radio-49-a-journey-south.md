# Touch / Chris Watson — A Journey South

This is the Antarctic ice recording we were looking for.

## Identity

- Label/site: Touch (`touch33.net`)
- Program: Touch Radio 49
- Artist: Chris Watson
- Title: *A Journey South*
- Date: 5 February 2010
- Duration: 50:21
- Stream: https://touchradio.org.uk/touchradio/Radio49.mp3
- Touch Radio page: https://touchradio.org.uk/touch_radio_49_chris_watson.html
- Touch catalogue page: https://touch33.net/news/touch_radio_49_chris_watson.html
- Chris Watson page: https://chriswatson.net/2010/02/05/touchradio-49-a-journey-south/

Watson made the recordings while travelling toward Antarctica for *Frozen Planet*. Contemporary descriptions of the program specifically identify the underwater hydrophone material as ice floes squeaking/creaking while rubbing against one another.

## Naming trap

“Touch 33” can mean the Touch label/site at `touch33.net`. The Antarctic recording itself is **Touch Radio 49**, not episode 33.

Literal Touch Radio 33 is AER, *Without Number*; see `touch-radio-33-aer.md`.
