# Owen Maresh sound-source notes

Goal: identify audio Owen Maresh actually used or explicitly paired with his mathematical / visual work, especially Internet Archive sources, without guessing.

## Verified from Owen's own site

- **Subsurface Currents of the Cosmic Ocean** explicitly says to listen along with the first moments of Brian Eno's *Music for Airports 1/1*.
  - http://owen.maresh.info/subsurface.html
- His home page points to **Peter Dilworth's synth music**, specifically “E7 Song 20” and “E4”.
  - http://owen.maresh.info/
- The same home page notes that the title of his piece **But We Have Learned Patience in Two Hundred Million Years** comes from *The Brain Eaters* and was sampled by The Orb in “Valley” on *Orbus Terrarum*.
  - http://owen.maresh.info/

These establish the kind of sound/music references he was using, but they are **not evidence of an Internet Archive source**.
